    Munich RSE Community

    Better Software - Better Research, also in Munich!

    Our group is the place to meet other research software engineers in Munich. Do you write code for research? It doesn’t matter if you just started or you are already an expert - This is your crowd!

    We aim for a meeting every two months, followed by dinner and networking. Our meetings include short talks about anything related to RSE (research data management, software development, teaching/training, and more), as well as open discussions on topics brought into the agenda by any attendee.

    A part of our group (with unfortunately many people missing) met at deRSE25.
