    Welcome!

    We are Research Software Engineers from Schleswig-Holstein. You are warmly invited to get in touch with us — we appreciate questions, discussions, and of course active participation.

    Meetings

    Our meetings are currently organized mainly by members of the Working Group on Research Software Engineering within the state initiative FDM.SH. However, everyone interested in research software, research data, or community building is very welcome to join.

    The meetings will take place online. We aim to convene on a quarterly basis, and additional meetings can be arranged upon request. If you would like to join, please contact us for access details or subscribe to our mailing list.

    FDM.SH

    FDM.SH is the state initiative for research data management in Schleswig-Holstein. It promotes collaborative solutions, supports coordination across institutions, and helps build shared structures for managing research data. Through joint efforts, expertise is pooled and made accessible to strengthen modern, FAIR-aligned research data practices throughout the state.

    Within FDM.SH, a dedicated Working Group on Research Software Engineering has formed — and it is from this group that the application for a Schleswig-Holstein chapter of de-RSE was submitted in December 2025.

    The working group aims to:

    • foster sustainable, transparent, and FAIR-oriented practices for research software and related data,
    • connect existing expertise across institutions in Schleswig-Holstein,
    • create exchange formats that facilitate networking between RDM experts, RSEs, and researchers.

    More information is available on the FDM.SH website.
